# Code of Conduct

We adopt the principles of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/): be welcoming, constructive, respectful, and considerate to contributors across backgrounds and experience levels. Harassment, discrimination, and abusive conduct are not acceptable.

For moderation concerns, contact project maintainers privately through GitHub channels. Do not publish personal or sensitive details in public issue threads. Maintainers may remove abusive comments or restrict participation to protect a constructive environment.
