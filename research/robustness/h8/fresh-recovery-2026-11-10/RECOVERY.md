**The energy number is an illustrative expenditure estimate in kcal/day.** The specification is `1.2 × RMR + incremental walking + net workout`, with Mifflin by default or `500 + 22 × FFM` by explicit choice. Walking uses steps above 2,000, estimated distance per step of `0.415 × height in metres`, and `0.5 kcal/kg/km`. Workout uses a whole-session MET preset, then subtracts the baseline assigned to that duration, floored at zero.

The 1.2 multiplier, 2,000-step allocation, height proxy and session METs are assumptions. Low-step days retain the baseline. Steps and workout activity may overlap, and the engine warns rather than reconciles. FFM is not SMM; equation agreement is not accuracy. The sum is not measured expenditure, a food allowance, a calibrated personal interval, or a clinically established target. Numerical bounds do not establish clinical eligibility.
